Backup & devices

Are my records backed up?

Yes, automatically, as long as your iPhone is signed in to iCloud. Pawtal saves everything to your own private iCloud account. You can check this in Pawtal under Settings → Backup & sync, which shows "Synced with iCloud" when it's working.

I got a new phone. How do I get my records back?

Sign in to the new phone with the same Apple Account, install Pawtal and open it. Your pets and records download from iCloud on their own. Give it a minute or two the first time.

Can I use Pawtal on more than one device?

Pawtal is designed for iPhone. You can also install it on an iPad or a Mac with Apple silicon, where it opens in a phone-sized window. As long as each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, they all show the same records, kept up to date automatically.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your Apple Account is all Pawtal needs. There's no separate sign-up or password.

Scanning paperwork

How does scanning work?

Go to Records → Scan → Scan document and hold your iPhone over the paper. It finds the edges, straightens the page and captures it automatically. You can scan several pages in one go before tapping Save. You can also choose a photo or a PDF instead. Pawtal reads the paper on your phone (nothing is uploaded) and fills in what it finds for you to check before saving. The original is kept in Records → Docs.

Scanning got something wrong or missed a detail.
  • Lay the paper flat in good light and fill the frame with it.
  • Printed papers work much better than handwriting.
  • Smart auto-fill works best on iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Other iPhones still find dates, weights and common vaccine names.
  • You can always correct any field before tapping Save.
My certificate lists several vaccines.

Pawtal picks up each one separately, with its own dates. On the review screen you'll see a card per vaccine. Untick any you don't want, fix anything that's wrong, or tap Add another vaccine if one was missed.

Where do my scanned documents go?

Every scan is saved in Records → Docs, where you can view it full-screen, rename it or share it. If a scan also filled in a vaccine or visit, the two are linked, so you can jump from one to the other.

Vaccines, treatments & medications

My pet just had a booster. How do I record it?

Tap Mark as given on the Home screen card, or in Records → Vaccines swipe the vaccine to the right and tap Given. Pawtal suggests the next due date using the same gap as last time (for example 1 or 3 years for rabies), which you can change. The previous dose stays in the history marked Renewed.

What does "Renewed" mean?

A newer dose of the same vaccine has been recorded, so this older one no longer counts as due or overdue. It's kept for the history and the Pet Passport.

How do I track flea, tick, heartworm or worming treatments?

Go to Records → Meds → Add flea, tick, heartworm or worming. Choose the type, the product and how often it's given (every month, every 3 months and so on), plus when you last gave it. Pawtal counts down to the next dose and shows it on the Home screen. When you give it, swipe right on the treatment (or use Mark as given on Home). To stop counting for a while, open it and turn on Paused.

Can Pawtal remind me to give a daily medication?

Yes (Pawtal Pro). Open the medication and turn on Remind me to give each dose, then pick the times, for example 8 AM and 8 PM. You'll get a notification every day at those times until the medication's end date. You can also set a refill reminder date on any medication.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders.
  1. Reminders for vaccines, appointments, treatments and medications are part of Pawtal Pro. Check Settings → Pawtal Pro in the app.
  2. Make sure Enable reminders is on in Pawtal's Settings.
  3. Make sure notifications are allowed in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Pawtal.
  4. A vaccine only gets reminders if it has a next due date, and a treatment only once you've logged a dose.
  5. To test, use Settings → Reminders → Send a test reminder.
When do reminders arrive?
  • Vaccines and appointments: at 9 AM, as far ahead as you choose in Settings → Reminders (set separately for each).
  • Treatments and medication refills: at 9 AM on the day they're due.
  • Daily doses: at the times you set on the medication.
  • Pet birthdays: at 9 AM on the day (free for everyone).

If you're busy, tap Remind me later on the Home screen card to get one extra reminder tomorrow, in 3 days or next week. The due date itself doesn't change.

What does the bell on the Home screen show?

Everything that needs your attention for the selected pet (overdue or due-soon items, appointments to confirm) and every reminder that's coming up, with the day and time it will arrive.

Widget

How do I add the Pawtal widget?

Open Pawtal once, then go to your Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Pawtal and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the area below the clock and choose Pawtal. The widget is part of Pawtal Pro.

The widget isn't up to date.

Open Pawtal for a moment. The widget refreshes whenever your records change in the app, and counts down by itself each day.

Your pets

How do I add another pet?

On the Home screen, tap your pet's name at the top and choose Add pet. The free plan includes one pet; Pawtal Pro lets you add as many as you like.

My pet has passed away.

We're so sorry. Open their profile and scroll to the bottom, then tap [name] has passed away. All of their records are kept, and you can still view them or share their Pet Passport, but every reminder stops, including birthday notifications. They also no longer count towards the free plan's one-pet limit.

How do I delete a pet?

Open the pet's profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete [name]. Pawtal shows exactly what will be removed before you confirm. This deletes the pet and all their records from every device signed in to your iCloud, and can't be undone.

Pawtal Pro

What's included in Pawtal Pro?

Unlimited pets; reminders for vaccines, appointments, flea/tick/heartworm treatments, daily medications and refills; the Home Screen and Lock Screen widget; and sharing the Pet Passport PDF. Records, scanning, documents, weight tracking, birthday notifications and iCloud backup are free for everyone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Pawtal, or in Pawtal tap Settings → Pawtal Pro → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Your records are never deleted when a subscription ends.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked.

In Pawtal, tap Settings → Pawtal Pro → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If it still doesn't work, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can my family use my subscription?

Yes. Pawtal Pro supports Apple Family Sharing, so members of your Family Sharing group get Pro at no extra cost.

Your data

Who can see my pet's records?

Only you, plus anyone you choose to share a PDF or document with. Records are stored on your devices and in your own iCloud, and we have no access to them. See our privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Delete each pet from its profile (Delete [name] at the bottom), which removes their records too. Or delete the app and then remove Pawtal's data from iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage → Pawtal.
